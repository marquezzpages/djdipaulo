LEGAL INFORMATION

Privacy.

This page describes how data may be processed while you browse the official website of Di Paulo.

Data and forms

This website does not have its own registration forms or user accounts. Booking inquiries use external services such as WhatsApp and email.

Calendar and Google Sheets

The public calendar displays only confirmed events. A Netlify function retrieves published concert information from Google Sheets. Do not include contracts, private contacts or financial details in the published sheet.

External links

WhatsApp, Instagram, Spotify, Beatport and SoundCloud links take you to third-party services and their respective privacy policies apply.

Spotify player

The player is only loaded after you select “Load player”. Spotify may process technical browsing data at that point.

Hosting technical logs

Netlify and its network providers may process technical access records, including IP address and time, for operation and security. This policy should be updated if analytics or additional cookies are enabled.
